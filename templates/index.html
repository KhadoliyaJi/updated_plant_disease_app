Dataset & training

Inside the training pipeline

The model was developed using the New Plant Diseases Dataset and trained as a multiclass image-classification problem.

📊 Dataset

  • Dataset: New Plant Diseases Dataset
  • Images: Approximately 87,000 RGB images
  • Classes: 38 healthy and diseased plant categories
  • Split: 80:20 training / validation

🧠 Model

  • Architecture: Customized Convolutional Neural Network
  • Framework: TensorFlow / Keras
  • Input: 128 × 128 × 3 RGB image
  • Output: 38-class prediction
  • Parameters: 1,251,654

Research

From project to research

“A Tailored Customized CNN for Crop Disease Detection”

Rahul Khadoliya · First Author & Researcher

This work presents a customized Convolutional Neural Network approach for automated crop disease detection. The research explores image-based diagnosis using deep learning and evaluates classification performance across 38 plant health categories.


The paper was presented at the International Conference on Network Intelligence & Emerging Computational Paradigms (ICNIECP 2026), Manipal University Jaipur, on 11 July 2026.
